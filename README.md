# 🌐 PrajaConnect

### A Citizen Engagement Platform for Transparent Governance

PrajaConnect is a web-based citizen engagement platform that connects citizens with politicians and local authorities. It provides a digital space where citizens can raise civic concerns, submit grievances, and communicate issues that affect their communities.

## ✨ Features

- 📢 **Civic Issue Reporting** – Citizens can report problems and concerns affecting their local communities.
- 📝 **Grievance Submission** – Submit complaints and grievances through the platform for further action.
- 🏛️ **Citizen–Politician Communication** – Helps citizens communicate their concerns directly with politicians.
- 📊 **Issue Management** – Enables submitted concerns to be viewed and managed efficiently.
- 🔍 **Issue Tracking** – Citizens can keep track of their submitted concerns and their progress.
- 👤 **Role-Based Access** – Separate functionality for citizens and politicians
- 🔐 **User Authentication** – Secure registration and login for platform users.
- 📍 **Community-Focused Governance** – Provides a digital channel for addressing local civic problems.
